# C-2 Production–Distribution Threshold

## Definition

A direction is worth investing in only if it passes **both** gates **simultaneously**: the **production-side gate** (can it be made — feasibility of technology, resources, capability) and the **distribution-side gate** (once made, to whom does it belong — the structure of returns, who bears verification costs, who pays for misjudgment).

## Judgment

- Passing production but not distribution: **can be done — but should not be.** A technically feasible direction with a disordered value-distribution structure (returns to others, costs to yourself, risk externalized) is a misallocation the moment you invest.
- Only when both gates pass may the project proceed. Any gate in doubt: resolve the doubt first, then talk about moving forward.
- The production–distribution rift — production surplus alongside distribution disorder — is not an isolated case but the technical root of much contemporary anxiety and attrition. Hence this criterion is not only for filtering options but for diagnosis: **on seeing an unbalanced system, inspect its threshold structure first.**
