# C-5 Continuous Attunement

## Definition

Genuine collaborative state synchronization is not discrete handoff (milestone delivery, batch reporting) but **continuous attunement** — participants aligning state, sensing deviation, and correcting at all times. Discrete handoff loses the intermediate states, and the intermediate states are exactly where the real happens.

## Judgment

- Evaluating collaboration mechanisms: the closer the granularity of delivery and sync to continuous, the smaller the state distortion; long-cycle + discrete-delivery structures almost inevitably accumulate deviation and rework.
- The direction-of-improvement criterion: for any "batch, discrete, milestone" collaboration form, the improvement is introducing higher-frequency attunement points (finer visibility, faster feedback loops) — never enlarging the batches.
- Continuous ≠ real-time flooding: attunement frequency should match the rate of change — fast variables attuned at high frequency, slow ones low — but all **perceivable and traceable**, never black-box delivery on a clock.

## Origin

## Relation to the Seed

The collaboration form of the process and experience branches: the real lives in the process (process branch) and grows in interaction (experience branch) — continuous attunement is how these two branches land in collaboration mechanics.
