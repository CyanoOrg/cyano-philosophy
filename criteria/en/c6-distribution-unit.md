# C-6 Distribution Unit

## Definition

Value distribution in a cognitive-collaboration network takes the **verifiable trajectory** as its base unit — not the product snapshot.

## Judgment

- Design and execute distribution by trajectory: whoever's verifiable trajectory (process records + verification receipts + differential history) is longer and more solid, to them the accumulation accrues.
- Snapshot-based distribution (allocating by ownership of the final product alone) is inadmissible: snapshots can be copied, misappropriated, forged; trajectories cannot — **whatever can be copied away should not be the distribution unit**.
- Linked with the C-4 verifiability ceiling: the verifiability of the trajectory caps the justice of distribution; trajectory segments that cannot be verified do not participate in distribution.

## Origin

2026-09 organizational-philosophy discussions: the design ruling on value-distribution mechanisms for cognitive co-creation networks (promoted to criterion rank 09-24, the second batch).

## Relation to the Seed

The confluence of the differential and sovereignty branches: the verifiable trajectory is the accumulated form of differential change (differential branch), and the claim over the trajectory is the claim over the real accumulation (sovereignty branch) — the distribution-unit criterion welds the two branches into the mechanism layer of value distribution.
