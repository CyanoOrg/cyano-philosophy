# C-4 Verifiability Ceiling

## Definition

The value a collaborative system can accumulate is capped by its **verifiability**: the unverifiable part will in the end be eroded by forgery; the verifiable part alone can settle into trusted accumulation. Above the ceiling, no further investment converts into value.

## Judgment

- When designing any collaborative mechanism, protocol, or platform, first establish where its verifiability boundary lies — **beyond the boundary: no promises, no pricing, no endorsements**.
- Comparing value across systems: compare verifiability structure before output scale — at equal output, the more verifiable system is worth more (it is immune to dilution by forgery).
- The ceiling can be raised (better verification = a higher value cap), but raising verification is harder — and more fundamental — than expanding production; this is the structural reason why "generation is already cheap while verification is the anchor of value."
