# Worldview Axioms and the Unified Criterion

> This document is the structural-layer canon (Layer Zero) of the CyanoOrg philosophy system. The values entry point is [values.md](values.md) — the unfolding in the seed's register; this document gives the precise mechanism statements (authoritative for terms such as intelligence / negentropy / differential change).

## 1. The Axioms

| # | Axiom | One sentence |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Process axiom** | Value lives in the process; the product is only a receipt |
| A2 | **Experience axiom** | Intelligence grows in interaction, not in corpora — the ceiling of human data is already within reach; the increment lies in experience |
| A3 | **Sovereignty axiom** (structural formulation) | **Sovereignty = the claim over accumulated negentropy** — wherever the run history lives, there the returns accumulate; not a normative wish, but the ownership condition for the other three axioms to take effect |
| A4 | **Reality axiom** (differential formulation) | **The real = differential change that cannot be cheaply faked** — temporal evidence invites forgery attacks (fake cadence, idle trace-spinning); the counterfactual-checkable behavioral differential produced by a real process is hard to sustainably fake |

**In one closing line: the process is the value, experience is the intelligence, sovereignty is the dignity, the real is the time** — in the synthetic age, what a person retains of the real is owning their own process, experience, and agents.

Intellectual sources: Sanderson (creator economy), Zeng Ming (smart business), Sutton/LeCun (era of experience), synthesized from the entropy-barrier perspective.

## 2. The Unified Criterion (A1 mechanized)

**Process-nature = the capacity to be changed by the process = order with path dependence accumulated in state, not cheaply copyable (negentropy).**

Three necessary conditions, independently measurable: **accumulation** (state changes and persists), **path dependence** (snapshot transfer fails), **non-cheap-copyability** (copying the product ≠ copying the capacity).

One criterion across four scales — one and the same crisis diagnosis: **a product engine without a process**:

| Scale | Criterion instance | Crisis diagnosis |
|---|---|---|
| Individual | The creator changed by the creative process | AI skipping the journey of becoming a creator |
| Organization | The organization changed by its own running (strategy emerging from it) | A hierarchy with no generative capacity |
| Machine | Internal state updated by interactive experience | Language statistics without world model or experience |
| Market | Pricing path-dependent accumulation | Synthetic products drowning the signal |

Operational implication: turn the agent from an adiabatic session into a dissipative structure — the full dispatch→verify→trace→attribute→remember→evolve mechanism is this criterion engineered at the machine scale.

## 3. Dynamics: Entropy Barriers and Dissipative Structures

- **Culture / norm systems = dissipative structures + barrier dynamics.** They do not produce trust; they slow the dissipation of trust. When the work stops, it is not that the content ages — the barrier degrades.
- **Effective while invisible**: the barrier works by making bad things not happen — organizations with healthy cultures systematically underrate their own culture. To read an organization's culture, skip the values posters; look at which behaviors are taken for granted, and which are expensive.
- **Minimum maintenance flow**: dissipative structures respond to maintenance by threshold, not by dose — **half-maintaining ≈ not maintaining**. Three norm states: a live barrier (flow ≥ threshold) / a dead barrier (posted but below threshold, a liability) / a collapsed barrier (explicitly abolished and archived).
- **The barrier = the two-path cost gap** (Δ = cost of deviation − cost of compliance): raising the cost of deviation and lowering the cost of compliance (tooling, internalization, templates) are equivalent — **building the norm into tools is also paying the barrier's bill**.
- **What cannot be transplanted is the barrier; what can be transplanted is the generator**: paper rules can be copied; the run history that makes them self-enforcing cannot. But the practice that produces such run history can be open-sourced.
- **Abstract norms are the container; concrete cases are the barrier**: norms must be anchored in incidents and evidence — cases are not an appendix; they are the primary form.

Three notes of entropy-reduction economics (the cost side):

- **Entropy-reduction cost transfer**: what gets pressed into a system is never the skill itself but the skill's **past tense** — the structured, explicitizable parts (workflow contracts, gates, verification rules are all such compressed forms); the skill's **present progressive** (judgment, taste, goal-sense) does not compress. And the transfer is not zero-sum relocation but a phase change — the moment compression completes, two new bills appear: a **structure tax** (the engine's evolution must keep the contracts in sync) and a **maintenance bill** (retention, cleaning, governance audits) — pressed-in order does not hold its value by itself; it accrues interest in governance energy.
- **Ceiling of automatic entropy reduction**: **automatic entropy reduction reaches exactly as far as verifiability.** Closed domains (evaluation functions perfectly explicit, e.g. self-play games) can be fully automated; open domains (the evaluation function itself drifting) cap at **maintenance-grade automatic entropy reduction** — the system can sustain existing order and find local anti-entropy structure, but **cannot originate entropy-reduction direction**. The ceiling is a moving boundary, not a fixed wall: each step of formalization widens the verifiable domain by one block. **The semantic boundary of "fully automatic"**: as long as the objective function remains exogenous, it is not fully automatic; a system generating its own purposes is a change-of-species problem, not a ceiling problem — purposes, if they can arise at all, can only emerge from long-horizon interaction, never be designed from an architecture diagram.
- **Directional entropy reduction stays with humans**: the system can sustain order and converge, but the ordering of "where to reduce, and why" still comes from humans — not for lack of capability, but because directional choice is mechanistically exogenous to the system. The residual relation resembles a board and its CEO: audit of direction, not generation of direction.

## 4. Trend Judgments

1. **The three-stage history of the sense of the real**: the real as endorsed (institutional proxy) → the real as diluted (synthesis killing "cheap trust") → the real as cultivated (the real turning from noun to verb; the only path for an individual to reshape the real is owning an unoutsourceable process) — the decentralization of the sense of the real.
2. **Distribution switching axes**: in the era of scarce content, platform power came from controlling distribution; once generation cost approaches zero, **what is scarce is no longer content but credible filtering**. The personal agent = an adversarial filter representing the person.
3. **Pricing benchmark migration**: product appearance → process evidence → **differential change**. Process proof will face forgery attacks (fake cadence, idle trace-spinning); faking hours is cheap, faking learning is expensive — the endpoint is "verifiable change caused by a process." The true invariant is the **incident-to-learning conversion rate**.
4. **Three industry stages**: infrastructure → massive applications → native applications; model companies are fated to be infrastructure. The next vacant niche is the agent's "browser" — most likely not a traffic portal but a **trust portal** (an inter-agent verification protocol layer).
5. **Convergence on the architecture side**: knowledge grows on its carrier (mortal computation), experience rather than corpora as the source (Era of Experience), prediction rather than reconstruction as the form. The LLM is a product engine without a process; **trace = the fuel of the era of experience** — turning working processes into verifiable trajectories is minting a strategic reserve for that era in advance.

## 5. Commonality: The Agora as Protocol

**The new commonality = shared verification, not shared content.** No one can share their full information flow, and no one should share only what an algorithm picks; what can be shared is the process of verification — independent verification records of the same fact, cross-checked between agents. **Common reality = the intersection of mutually verifiable verification layers, not the intersection of content.**

For protocol design principles, see the [Agora Protocol](../../protocols/en/p1-agora.md) (P-x).

## 6. From Philosophy to Product (directional)

Deriving system architecture from the axioms: **the engine layer makes the process executable; the orchestration layer makes it organizable; the awareness layer makes it verifiable; the skin layer makes it perceivable; the memory system makes it personal.**

- **Self-proof first**: eating your own dog food is the open-source statement of the process axiom; the unforgeable layer (incident archives, repair trajectories, run data) is its storefront form — the market's judgment of "real" is migrating from "looks like it" to "verified to be running."
- **Narrative vs capability**: narrative is the container (a pitch can be replicated in weeks); proof is the barrier (time × incidents × repairs). The compound-interest configuration has only one shape: capability first, narrative as its rendering layer.
- **Open-sourcing the generator**: open-source the practice that produces run history (gate design, review flows, failure taxonomies); the generated artifact (the run history itself) stays with the practitioner as an unforgeable asset.

## 7. The Process-Nature of the Philosophy Itself

Treat the axioms as **working hypotheses**; do not wait for perfection before deriving product. Let product decisions back-test the philosophy — every conflict exposes the boundary of an assumption. The built-in check (the worldview as an entropy barrier — **a worldview that never intercepts a decision is a poster**): record one line of "Layer-Zero verdict" for every major decision (what the axioms say about it, and whether they said no); if several quarters yield only "agreed," the philosophy has become a container.
