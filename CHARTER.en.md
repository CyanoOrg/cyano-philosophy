# CHARTER — Revision Governance (skeleton v0.1)

> This charter governs revisions to cyano-philosophy. The manifesto governs itself by its own method: the adjudication mechanism cites our own adjudication-layer philosophy.
> **Terms**: in this charter, "owner" denotes the CyanoOrg maintainer (the final-adjudication role) — the holder of final revision authority over the philosophical canon.

## 1. Tiered Revision Thresholds

| Layer | Directory | Revision condition |
|---|---|---|
| Canon · values | axioms/values.md | proposal + rebuttal period + owner ruling |
| Canon · axioms | axioms/worldview.md | proposal + rebuttal period + at least one product proof |
| Criteria | criteria/ | proposal + proof on record |
| Protocols | protocols/ | proposal + product-mapping verification |
| Evolution archive | evolution/ | append-only: archive only, never rewrite history |

## 2. Red Lines

- **The seed–branches unfolding**: the sense of the real is the seed of empathy; the axioms are its structural statement — the seed does not swallow the branches; the branches do not pretend to be the root
- **Anti-dogma**: every axiom must maintain at least one live product mapping (mapping evidence lives in each product repository's philosophy layer; citation direction is product → philosophy; this repository admits no references to specific projects); an axiom losing its mapping enters "awaiting test" status
- **The working-hypothesis posture**: rendering anything "undiscussable" is a violation

## 3. Community Contributions

- Translation: a new language must first translate the glossary (wiki/); main texts only after review
- Discussion and proposals: via issues; canonical revision authority rests with CyanoOrg
- Citation convention: citations must carry proposition numbers and version anchors (e.g., A1@v1.0)
